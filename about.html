How it works

A plain explanation of how a business gets a verified page, badge and QR code, how we check it, and where it stops.

For a business

  • 1. You ask for a pageSend us a WhatsApp message with your business name. You do not upload your ID or tax papers to the website.
  • 2. We check you ourselvesWe match the name on your payment account to your business name, call a number we found independently, and look at your documents on a video call or in person.
  • 3. You get a page, a badge and a QR codeYour page shows your official details, a Confirmed tag and the date we last checked. You download the badge as a picture.
  • 4. You show it to buyersPut the badge and QR code on your Facebook page, WhatsApp status and invoices.

How we check a business

A page is marked Being checked until we have done the checks ourselves. Only then does it become Confirmed, with the date we last checked.

We look at your documents, but we do not keep copies of them, and the website does not store ID or tax papers. If a business changes its payment details, its page goes back to Being checked until we confirm the change.

For a buyer

Scan the QR code, or open the link, and compare the details on the page with the ones you were sent. Then check the web address. It must be exactly the address printed under the QR code. A copy of a page on a similar-looking address can show different details.

What the badge means

It means that on the date shown, we checked that these are the business’s official details. It does not mean the business is perfect or that we guarantee any sale.

Anyone can copy a badge picture, which is why the address under the QR code matters. A badge only counts if the address it opens is the real one.

Checking a detail

The “Check a detail” page compares a number against an official record we hold. When a detail is almost the same as an official one, it shows both side by side and marks the digits that differ. It may be a typing mistake or a deliberate copy, so confirm with the institution before paying.

What VeriChecker cannot do

It can only tell you whether a detail is on an official record. It cannot tell you whether a request for money is genuine, and it cannot confirm that money has reached an account.

If a message or call you didn’t start asks you to pay, contact the business yourself using a number from its official website or your card or statement.
