Know who you are paying.

For schools, shops and companies that send money to suppliers, institutions and each other.

When to use it

  • A new supplier sends their detailsCheck their mobile money number or bank account before the first payment.
  • A message says “we’ve changed our account”This is the most common trick. Check the new details before you send anything.
  • You are paying a bill, a fee or a school accountCheck the paybill, merchant code or account number first.

What it does, and what it doesn’t

It does

  • Compare an account, phone number, payment code or name with the institution’s official record
  • Warn you when a detail is almost the same as a real one
  • Give you a result in seconds, any time of day

It doesn’t

  • Confirm that money has reached your account. Check your own balance for that
  • Tell you whether a request for money is genuine
  • Replace calling the institution when something still feels wrong

Check a detail
VeriChecker can only confirm that a detail is on an institution’s official list. It cannot tell whether a request for money is genuine. If a message or call you didn’t start asks you to pay, contact the institution yourself using a number from its official website or your card or statement.
