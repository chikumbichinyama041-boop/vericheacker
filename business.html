Show your buyers you are real.

For sellers, shops and service providers whose buyers worry about scams.

Why get a page

  • Scammers copy your photos and nameA buyer cannot tell which page is the real one. Your page gives them somewhere to check.
  • Buyers get “we’ve changed our account” messagesWith your page, they can compare the details they were sent with your official ones.
  • Trust sellsA buyer who can check you in ten seconds is more likely to pay.

What you get

  • Your own pageYour official payment and contact details, a Confirmed tag and the date we last checked.
  • A badge you can downloadA picture for your Facebook page, WhatsApp status and invoices.
  • A QR code and linkBuyers scan it and land on your page. The web address is printed under the code so they can see it is the real one.

What it does, and what it doesn’t

It does

  • Show buyers that we checked your business and your details on a date
  • Give buyers a place to compare the details they were sent
  • Go back to “Being checked” if your payment details change

It doesn’t

  • Stop someone from copying the badge picture. Buyers must check the web address
  • Guarantee a sale or confirm that a payment has arrived
  • Replace calling you when something still feels wrong

How we check you

We match the name on your payment account to your business name, call a number we found independently, and look at your documents on a video call or in person. We do not keep copies, and you do not upload your ID or tax papers to this website.
