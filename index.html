Check before you pay.

Schools, shops and companies lose money to fake “we changed our account” messages. VeriChecker compares an account number, phone number or payment code with the institution’s official record, so you know it is real before the money moves.

Check a detail

Hunt the scam before it hunts you.

Who uses VeriChecker

  • SchoolsCheck the fee account before parents pay, and before the bursar sends money out.
  • ShopsCheck a new supplier’s number or till code before you send the first payment.
  • CompaniesCheck the account on an invoice that says the details have changed.

How it works

  • Choose the institutionThe bank, mobile money provider, school or company the detail is supposed to belong to.
  • Enter the detail you were givenAn account number, phone number, paybill or merchant code, or the account holder name.
  • See whether it matchesYou get a clear result, and a warning if the detail looks like a copy of a real one.
VeriChecker can only confirm that a detail is on an institution’s official list. It cannot tell whether a request for money is genuine. If a message or call you didn’t start asks you to pay, contact the institution yourself using a number from its official website or your card or statement.
